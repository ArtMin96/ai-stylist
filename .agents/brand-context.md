## Brand
- **Name**: Garderi (say GAR-der-ee; from the French *garde-robe*, "the keeper of clothes"). Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-14; recorded as DEC-85, which resolves OQ-01.
- **Category**: Consumer mobile app (iOS + Android): AI personal styling and digital wardrobe.
- **Description**: A personal stylist that knows your proportions, your closet and your day: outfits from the clothes you own, for the weather and the plans you have.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch (in development; store launch planned at phase P14).
- **Website**: N/A yet. garderi.com, garderi.app and garderi.ai were unregistered on 2026-10-14; the owner registers them.

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Adults and teens 16+ who own more clothes than they use and want help deciding what to wear every day. Personas (planning/00): wardrobe organizers, decision-fatigued daily dressers, style developers, and fit-sensitive shoppers underserved by standard sizing. Global, English-first. **Primary** (owner-confirmed 2026-10-18): the daily dresser; favour them when design decisions conflict.
- **Key Problem**: A full closet and nothing to wear: daily outfit decisions that ignore the weather, the day's plans and what is actually owned; clothes forgotten at the back of the closet; not knowing how pieces work together or on you.
- **Their Language**: Not yet collected (no interviews or reviews in the repo). Hypotheses to verify: "nothing to wear", "what should I wear today", "outfit of the day / OOTD", "capsule wardrobe", "cost per wear", "shop my closet".

## Positioning
- **Positioning line** (owner-confirmed 2026-10-18): Garderi suggests what to wear only from clothes you already own, explains every suggestion in plain words, and never sells you anything.
- **Differentiation**: Styles only what you already own (not a shop); reads your day (weather, forecast, holidays, occasion); explains every suggestion with real reasons; treats privacy as a feature and never judges your body. Outfits show as a collage of your own pieces; the stylized 3D avatar with your proportions comes later, pending the DEC-76 go/no-go.
- **Competitors**: Whering, Indyx, Acloset, Cladwell (closet apps); Doji, Doppl, BetterMirror (avatar and try-on).
- **Market Position**: Premium (3-day full trial, a real Free tier, then Essentials $4.99, Plus $9.99, Pro $19.99 per month; prices are hypotheses).

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words** (owner-confirmed and binding, 2026-10-18): calm, perceptive, warm, crafted, honest.
- **Tone**: Conversational but composed. Warm, never silly. Serious about trust, light in delivery.
- **Voice Admires**: Not stated yet (ask the owner). Visual references used in the identity brief: Diptyque, Casa da Música, Aesop.

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values** *(proposed, drawn from the planning docs)*: Trust is the product (privacy by default, DEC-29); Honesty (no "exact digital twin" claims, explanations come from real reasons); Respect for every body (no body judgement, no attractiveness scoring); Wear what you own (use over consumption; closet gaps are information, not shopping links); Craft (made with care).
- **Mission** *(proposed)*: Help people get dressed with confidence every day, using the clothes they already own.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal** *(proposed)*: Launch v1 on the App Store and Google Play and grow weekly "dressed-by-app" users.
- **Key Metrics**: Weekly dressed-by-app users (the north-star metric, planning/00); trial-to-paid conversion and retention *(proposed)*.
